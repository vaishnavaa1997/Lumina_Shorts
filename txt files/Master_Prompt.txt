    - `'terrainHistorical'`: Pure physical mountain/elevation relief (`Esri Shaded Relief`, zero modern roads/labels) graded in warm historical parchment.
    - `'streetCanals'` / `'darkStreet'`: High-detail city street, river, and canal tiles (`CartoDB`).
    - `'parchment'` / `'relief'`: 2.5D extruded vector landmass polygons.
